Healthcare Performance & Operations Analysis Dashboard

A multi-page Power BI analytical platform built on 55,500 patient records across 10 major U.S. hospitals—converting clinical, financial, and operational measures into interactive, decision-ready intelligence.

Power BI DAX Power Query

Project Overview

Designed to answer critical strategic healthcare questions, this analytical platform consolidates complex clinical, operational, and financial data into interactive, executive-ready views.

By leveraging dynamic DAX measures and parameter switches, hospital administrators can seamlessly evaluate patient demographics, medical condition prevalence, treatment cost structures, insurer billing variations, and geographic performance without losing macro context.

Strategic Business Value: Pinpoints operational bottlenecks in patient Length of Stay (LOS), identifies treatment cost variances across major insurers, and optimizes regional facility capacity via GIS geospatial mapping.
