Merchandise Sales & Customer Analysis Dashboard

An end-to-end e-commerce analytics platform built on merchandise sales data to systematically evaluate product performance, logistics, and customer sentiment.

Power BI DAX Power Query

Project Overview

Designed to evaluate the business performance of a creator-led product launch, this multi-page Power BI dashboard systematically addresses product profitability, geographic order distribution, buyer demographics, shipping economics, and customer review sentiment.

Strategic Business Impact: Identifies top-performing product categories by revenue while isolating low-rated items for quality control, quantifies international shipping cost drag to guide fulfillment expansion, and maps customer demographics for targeted social ad campaigns.
